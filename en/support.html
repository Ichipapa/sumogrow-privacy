FAQ

How do I play?

Drag your finger on the screen and your wrestler moves in that direction.

Absorb Mode (60 seconds): Bump into wrestlers with a sweaty, scared face (green outline) to absorb them and grow heavier. Wrestlers with an angry face and a "!" above their head (orange outline) are bigger than you. Hit one and your weight is cut in half. Staying outside the ring makes you shrink little by little.

Push Mode (60 seconds): Everyone is the same size. When you collide, the one with more speed sends the other flying. Push a wrestler you hit (spiral above its head, green outline) out of the ring to score a point. If you leave the ring yourself, the game ends right away.

What is "Continue"?

When you get pushed out in Push Mode, you can watch a video ad to keep playing from the center of the ring. You can use it once per game. If you bought "Remove Ads," you can use it without watching a video.

I want to remove ads

Buy "Remove Ads" (one-time purchase) at the bottom of the title screen. Full-screen ads after games will no longer appear.

Ads came back after I changed devices

Tap "Restore" at the bottom of the title screen. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.
Note: Best records are saved on your device and do not move to a new device.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please use Apple's "Report a Problem" page.
