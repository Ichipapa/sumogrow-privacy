Sumo Bump Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "Sumo Bump" (Japanese title: ぶつかり力士; the "App") handles user information.

1. Information the App collects

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. There is no account registration.

The following information is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer:

  • Best records (wrestlers absorbed, max weight, and wrestlers pushed out)
  • Settings such as the last mode you chose and sound on/off

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To deliver ads, Google may collect and use the following information:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in your device settings
  • Device information such as your IP address, device model, and OS version
  • Usage information such as how many times ads are shown and whether they are tapped

This information is used to deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud, and show ads that are more relevant to you. For details on how Google handles this information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

3. Allowing and stopping tracking

After you finish your first game, the App asks for permission to track through the iPhone/iPad "Allow Tracking" prompt. If you do not allow it, you can still use the App normally, and you will see ads that are not personalized.

You can change this at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland can change their ad consent choices at any time with the "Privacy" button at the bottom of the App's title screen.

4. In-app purchase

The "Remove Ads" purchase is made through Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple, and the developer does not receive it.

5. Sharing information with third parties

The developer does not sell or share your information with third parties, except for the information collected by the advertising service described above (Google) and where required by law.

6. Children

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.
